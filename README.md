README.md is the most important file. Acts as an executive summary explaining the project's objective, methodology, key findings, and final results in plain business English.


notebooks/: Contains Jupyter Notebooks (.ipynb) showing step-by-step analysis, data cleaning, backtesting, or risk modeling with embedded charts.

src/ or scripts/: Reusable Python or R scripts for data fetching, optimization, or risk calculations (e.g., VaR, Sharpe ratio calculations, asset allocation models).

data/: Sample or anonymized datasets (avoid uploading proprietary client data or restricted market feeds).

outputs/: Saved PDF reports, CSV summaries, or high-resolution PNG charts that clients can view instantly without running code.
